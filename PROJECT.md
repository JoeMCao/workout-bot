# Project

Workout Bot

# Aliases

Workout Bot, workout-bot

# Purpose

Provide a conversational workout coach with durable, structured training history so recommendations can reflect completed sessions, weekly plans, readiness, pain signals, and WHOOP recovery data.

# Area

Health

# In Scope

Workout and activity logging, exercise and set history, weekly training planning and review, readiness and recovery context, WHOOP synchronization, the training dashboard, and the REST, OpenAPI, MCP, and Custom GPT interfaces that support those workflows.

# Out of Scope

General-purpose health or medical advice, nutrition tracking, social fitness features, and unrelated personal-data or productivity systems.

# Status

Active
